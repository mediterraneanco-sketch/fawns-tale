# The Fawn’s Tale — შვლის ნუკრის ნაამბობი — حكايات من عيون الغزال
**Author / Narrative Adaptation:** Vakhtang Imerlishvili (ვახტანგ იმერლიშვილი)  
**Inspired by:** Vazha-Pshavela (ვაჟა-ფშაველა)

---

### About the Edition
**“The Fawn’s Tale”** is a contemporary narrative adaptation of Vazha-Pshavela’s poignant Georgian classic, told from the perspective of a young fawn — a timeless story about the voice of nature, the right to life, and human conscience.
